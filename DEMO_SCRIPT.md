# AlnonAI 2.0 — 90-second demo script

**0:00–0:10 | Opening**

"AlnonAI began as a support navigator for people and families affected by alcohol-related harm. Version 2.0 keeps that purpose and adds source-grounded healthcare information and safer referral pathways."

**0:10–0:20 | Discreet entry**

Show the calculator-styled PIN screen. Explain that the PIN is an app-level access gate, not database encryption.

**0:20–0:35 | Language and assistant**

Unlock the app, switch between English, Hindi and Telugu, then ask a health-information question. Show the retrieved answer and source link. Explain that the app uses local lexical retrieval and will say when it cannot find a source.

**0:35–0:48 | Health literacy and family wellbeing**

Open Health information and show appointment questions. Open Family wellbeing and show safe, non-judgmental support guidance.

**0:48–1:02 | Practical support directory**

Search the directory and show official 112, Tele-MANAS, NALSA, National Scholarship Portal and myScheme/National Career Service starting points. Explain that local NGOs and rehabilitation centres must be verified before being relied on.

**1:02–1:15 | Safety workflow**

Show emergency call links, trusted contacts and the safety checklist. Demonstrate the manual location-message preview only with a dummy location and explicit consent. Emphasise that nothing is sent automatically and no GPS is accessed.

**1:15–1:30 | Technical design and limitations**

"The prototype uses Streamlit, SQLite, a PBKDF2 PIN verifier and a local BM25-style retrieval pipeline with source metadata. It is not a clinical tool. It still needs clinician review, independent Hindi and Telugu review, accessibility testing and security assessment before real-world use."
