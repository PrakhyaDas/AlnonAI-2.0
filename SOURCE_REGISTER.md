## Curation rules

- Prefer official government, WHO or recognised public-health sources.
- Store the source URL, title, language, topic and review date with every knowledge chunk.
- Do not invent local provider names or phone numbers.
- Re-check current contact details, eligibility, availability and service coverage before public use.
- Have a qualified clinician review all health content and fluent speakers review Hindi and Telugu before competition claims of clinical or translation quality.
